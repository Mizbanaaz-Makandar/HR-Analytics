# HR Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end HR analytics project analyzing workforce data across **50,000 employees** in **10+ departments** to uncover attrition drivers, promotion bottlenecks, and compensation gaps — and translate those findings into concrete retention recommendations for leadership.

## Project Overview

Employee attrition is expensive, and most organizations don't know *why* people are actually leaving until it's too late. This project builds an interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboard that goes beyond surface-level attrition rates to identify **who** is leaving, **when** in their tenure they leave, and **why** — using income bracket, tenure stage, promotion velocity, and department-level salary equity as the core lenses.

##  Key Objectives

- Identify which employee segments carry the highest attrition risk
- Determine whether attrition is driven by compensation, career stagnation, or performance
- Surface promotion velocity trends across tenure groups
- Flag compensation equity gaps across departments
- Deliver actionable, leadership-ready recommendations — not just charts

## Key Findings

| Insight | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Highest attrition segment** | Mid-level earners ($10K–$30K) — 5,570 employees — not the lowest earners, as commonly assumed |
| **Tenure risk window** | Employees at 3–5 years tenure show the highest attrition, pointing to career stagnation rather than performance issues |
| **Compensation gap** | $15–$16 salary hike gap between Software/R&D ($786–$794) and Hardware/HR ($801–$802) departments |
| **Promotion bottleneck** | Sharp drop-off in promotion velocity at the 6–10 year tenure mark (11,542 employees) — a mid-career retention risk |

##  Dashboard KPIs

- Average Performance Rating: **2.49 / 5**
- Average Salary Hike: **24.64%**
- Average Tenure: **11 years**
- Average Hourly Rate: **$114.45**

The dashboard allows stakeholders to filter by department, tenure band, and income bracket to drill into any of the above trends interactively.

##  Recommendations Delivered

1. **Mid-income retention program** — targeted interventions for the $10K–$30K earner segment, the group most at risk of leaving
2. **Promotion velocity review** — address the 6–10 year tenure bottleneck before it drives further attrition
3. **Salary equity audit** — close the identified gap between Software/R&D and Hardware/HR compensation tracks
